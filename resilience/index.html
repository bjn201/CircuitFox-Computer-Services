How long could a battery run your devices?

Use the battery's capacity in watt-hours and the combined average consumption of your devices. This is an estimate, not a measured result.

Estimated runtime
8.0 hours
800 Wh of usable energy

Runtime = capacity × usable percentage ÷ average load. Inverter standby consumption, temperature and battery condition can change the result. Fridges cycle on and off; check their average use and starting surge. A battery must also support the device's operating and surge power. Solar input is excluded.
