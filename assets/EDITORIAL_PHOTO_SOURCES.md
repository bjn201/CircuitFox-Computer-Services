## Brand constraints

- **PC repair imagery:** hardware only, no hands or people.
- **Power & Resilience:** home-office and professional business continuity, not camping or survivalist scenes.
- Editorial pictures: use a 16:9 crop with `object-fit: cover`; actual product pictures should remain uncropped with `object-fit: contain`.
- Before publishing future changes, confirm each photo is still licensed and loads successfully. The fallback image handler in `assets/site.js` uses real local product photos if the third-party host is unavailable.
