Returns & refunds

Contact us about a return

Send your order reference, the item and whether you are cancelling or reporting a fault. Contact us for the return address and suitable shipping instructions, particularly for batteries. Contacting us does not require you to obtain permission to exercise your cancellation rights.

Email info@circuitfoxtech.com or call 07410 582082.

Changing your mind

For standard goods bought online, tell us within 14 days after delivery that you want to cancel. You then have a further 14 days to send the goods back. You may inspect them as you would in a shop; unnecessary handling can reduce the refund.

Refunds include standard outbound delivery and use the original payment method. We refund within 14 days of receiving the goods or evidence that you sent them back, whichever is earlier. You pay return delivery for a change of mind.

Faulty or misdescribed goods

Your statutory rights apply to new and pre-owned goods. Contact us to arrange the appropriate refund, repair or replacement and reasonable return costs. These rights are separate from change-of-mind returns.

Cancellation message

You can copy this into an email: “I cancel my order for [item], ordered on [date], received on [date]. Order reference: … Name: … Address: … Date: …” You can also send any other clear cancellation statement.
