A practical tech checklist for a power cut

Keep essential electronics manageable with a small, tested plan.

Choose priorities

Decide which devices matter first: a phone, lighting and a way to access useful information may take priority over a desktop computer. Charge portable batteries and keep the matching leads together.

Check connectivity assumptions

Powering a router only helps if the other equipment and upstream service remain available. A fibre connection may also require an optical terminal. Mobile coverage is another dependency, so keep important information available offline.

Test the kit

Try each charger and device together while the mains is available. Note estimated consumption and test duration. Keep instructions somewhere accessible without internet access and make sure other household members can use the setup.

Review periodically

Recharge and inspect the equipment as its manufacturer recommends. Update contact lists and offline documents, and replace damaged leads. Keep the plan focused on a few useful tasks so battery energy lasts and the kit stays easy to use.
