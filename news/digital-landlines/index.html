Digital landlines: check your power plan

What the switch means for calls during a power cut.

Explainer · Reviewed October 2026

The official guidance

Ofcom explains that digital landlines rely on electricity and may need an alternative power arrangement during a power cut. Its guidance was updated on 26 August 2026.

Read the source: Ofcom →

What to ask your provider

  • Which equipment in my home needs to stay powered?
  • What arrangements are available if I rely on my landline?
  • Will my alarm or other connected equipment work after the change?

Our take

Do not start by buying a battery at random. Ask the provider to explain the complete setup, then test any agreed solution. Keep a usable alternative way to call if your circumstances allow.
