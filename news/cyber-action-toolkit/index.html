A useful starting point for cyber security

A manageable way for a small business to begin.

Explainer · Reviewed October 2026

The official guidance

The National Cyber Security Centre’s Cyber Action Toolkit provides practical actions for sole traders and small businesses.

Read the source: NCSC →

Our take

Start with the toolkit rather than trying to solve everything in one evening. Choose a short task, finish it and record what changed. Put another session in your diary if that helps you keep moving.

Keep the work relevant to the devices and accounts you actually use. If you run a business from a home PC, include that PC in the plan.

Make it useful

Before changing settings, make sure you can recover access to the account. Keep a note of unresolved questions and ask for help when a task is unclear.
