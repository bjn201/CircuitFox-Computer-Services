Custom Builds

Four example builds, tailored to your needs. Enquire for a personalised parts list and quote.

Standard Build

Esports, school/work, light creation, 1080p gaming.

  • CPU: Ryzen 5 5600 / i5-12400
  • GPU: RTX 3060 / RX 7600
  • RAM: 16GB DDR4
  • Storage: 1TB NVMe SSD
  • Motherboard: B550/B660
  • PSU: 550–650W 80+ Bronze
  • Case: Compact ATX, good airflow

Gaming Lite

1440p gaming, streaming, light editing; quiet and efficient.

  • CPU: Ryzen 5 7600X / i5-13600K
  • GPU: RTX 4070 / RX 7800 XT
  • RAM: 32GB DDR5
  • Storage: 1TB NVMe SSD (Gen4)
  • Motherboard: B650 or value Z790
  • PSU: 650–750W 80+ Gold
  • Case: Airflow mid tower
  • Cooler: 240mm AIO or premium air

Gaming Plus

Serious 1440p/high-refresh gaming and creator work.

  • CPU: Ryzen 7 9800X3D
  • GPU: RTX 3090 (24GB)
  • RAM: 32GB DDR5
  • Storage: 1TB NVMe SSD (Gen4) + optional 2TB add-on
  • Motherboard: MSI B850 PRO
  • PSU: 850W 80+ Gold
  • Case: Premium airflow tower
  • Cooler: 360mm AIO or top-tier air

Extreme Build

Top 1440p / entry 4K, heavy content creation, AI/ML tinkering.

  • CPU: Ryzen 9 7900X/7950X / i7-14700K
  • GPU: RTX 4080 Super / RX 7900 XTX
  • RAM: 32–64GB DDR5
  • Storage: 2TB NVMe SSD (Gen4) + scratch SSD optional
  • Motherboard: X670E / Z790 performance
  • PSU: 850–1000W 80+ Gold/Platinum (ATX 3.0)
  • Case: Premium tower, excellent thermals
  • Cooler: 360mm AIO or custom loop-ready
