RayNeo Air 3S Pro — Smart Glasses

The RayNeo Air 3S Pro is a pair of glasses that acts as a personal screen when connected to a compatible device. It's handy for watching films or using a handheld without carrying a separate monitor. The main snag is compatibility: some phones and laptops need extra adapters. A good fit if you want a portable screen and check your device first.

Meta Quest 3 — VR Headset

The Meta Quest 3 is a standalone VR headset with controllers and mixed-reality features. It's good for games, fitness apps and trying VR without building a dedicated PC setup. The standard strap can get uncomfortable during longer sessions. A sensible all-round choice if you want one headset for several kinds of VR.
