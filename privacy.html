Privacy

Circuit Fox Tech is the data controller responsible for the enquiry and order information described below.

This notice explains personal information used for CircuitFox Tech enquiries and shop orders. For privacy questions or requests:

Email info@circuitfoxtech.com or call 07410 582082.

Enquiries and orders

The quote form opens your email app. It sends nothing until you send the email. Your name, email and message are used to answer your enquiry and discuss the service you request.

Stripe handles checkout and payment information on its own website. Order contact details, items, delivery address and payment status are used to process purchases, arrange delivery, provide support and handle refunds. Do not email card numbers or security codes.

Why information is used

Enquiry and order information is used to take steps at your request and perform a contract. Records may also be needed for legal accounting obligations and legitimate interests in resolving complaints, preventing fraud and protecting the service.

Service providers and retention

Email, payment, delivery and website hosting providers handle relevant information to provide those services. The website is served through Cloudflare; its source is hosted on GitHub. Provider processing may involve countries outside the UK and the safeguards described in their privacy notices.

Records are retained only as needed for the enquiry, order, support, legal record-keeping and potential claims; these purposes determine the retention period.

Website and payment services

The site’s own code does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting and external services have their own practices: Cloudflare, GitHub and Stripe.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or object to processing. Contact us above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
